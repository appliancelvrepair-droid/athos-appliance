Sound Familiar?

Common Problems We Fix Every Day

If your appliance is on this list — don't wait for it to get worse. Most of these are same-day fixes.

🧊 Refrigerator

  • Not cooling or freezing food in the fridge section
  • Water leaking inside or onto the floor
  • Ice maker stopped working
  • Loud buzzing or clicking noises
  • Frost buildup in the freezer

🫧 Washer

  • Won't drain or finish the cycle
  • Drum won't spin or agitates weakly
  • Leaks from the door or underneath
  • Shakes and bangs on spin cycle
  • Won't start at all

🔥 Oven & Range

  • Oven not heating or heating unevenly
  • Burner clicks but won't ignite
  • Temperature way off the setting
  • Error codes on the display
  • Door won't close or seal properly

🍽️ Dishwasher & More

  • Dishes come out still dirty
  • Water stays at the bottom
  • Dryer runs but produces no heat
  • Microwave sparks or won't heat
  • Strange smells or tripped breakers
