Good to Know

Common problems — and what to do

A few things we hear every week. Tap a question to see our answer.

My fridge runs warm, but the freezer is fine
Most often it's a failed evaporator fan, blocked vents or a defrost problem — all very fixable. Keep the door closed as much as you can and book a visit: this one rarely fixes itself and usually gets more expensive if you wait.
The washer stopped with water still inside
Usually a clogged drain filter, a worn pump or a kinked hose. Don't force the door — note any error code on the display and call us. In most cases it's a same-visit repair.
My oven takes forever to heat up
A weak igniter (gas) or a failing bake element (electric) is the classic cause. If you smell gas, turn the oven off and call right away — otherwise it's a routine, affordable fix.
The dryer tumbles but there's no heat
Typically the heating element, a thermal fuse or a clogged vent. Check the lint trap first — if that's clean, book a diagnosis and we'll pinpoint it on the spot.
How much will my repair cost?
Every visit starts with a full diagnosis and an exact flat-rate quote. You approve the price before any work begins — if you decide not to repair, you only pay for the diagnosis. No surprises, ever.
Do you work on premium brands like Sub-Zero or Miele?
Yes — premium and built-in appliances are welcome. We service Viking, Thermador, Sub-Zero, Wolf, Miele, Bosch, KitchenAid and all major brands across the Las Vegas valley.
